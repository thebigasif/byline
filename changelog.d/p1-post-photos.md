### Added

- An author's `photo` shows at the top of their author page and as a small round photo beside their name in bylines, post cards and author lists. The photo is a file beside the author's file, so the author lives in a folder such as `authors/lena-vogt/index.md` with `lena.png` beside it.
- A post's lead image shows on its cards on author pages, lists and related posts, loaded when it nears the screen. Home page cards have no image yet, because `khalid serve` answers with the home page before any image has its sizes.

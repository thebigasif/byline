### Added

- `[seo.publisher]` in `site.toml` says who is behind the site, a company or a person, with a logo, profiles, contact details and a founding date, for every page's schema graph (audit G11).
- `[[seo.locations]]` adds a shop, office or clinic with its address and opening hours to the schema graph.
- `[[seo.schema]]` writes one schema.org node, such as a `Course`, for every post in a collection, with properties filled from the post.

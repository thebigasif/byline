### Added

- Under each post, "Posts that link here" lists the site's posts whose bodies link to it, newest first (`linked_mentions` under `[reader]`). It comes from the links the related posts already read, so `khalid serve` shows the same on a saved post's early page.
- A link in a post's body to another page of the site shows that page's summary while it is pointed at or focused, read once from the page's own description by the reader script; Escape hides it. With scripts off it is a plain link, and pages carry nothing extra for it (`link_previews` under `[reader]`) (G19).

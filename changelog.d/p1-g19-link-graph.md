### Added

- Under each post, "Posts that link here" lists the site's posts whose bodies link to it, newest first (`linked_mentions` under `[reader]`).
- Each link in a post's body to another post carries that post's summary, and the reader script shows it while the link is pointed at or focused; Escape hides it. With scripts off the link is a plain link (`link_previews` under `[reader]`). Both come from the links the related posts already read, so `khalid serve` shows the same on a saved post's early page (G19).

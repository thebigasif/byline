### Added

- `khalid-seo` has a typed schema builder for every content type in plan section 5.9: `SoftwareApplication`, `Event`, `Dataset`, `ItemList`, `Course`, `Book`, `PodcastEpisode` and `ClaimReview` join `Product`, each a `SchemaBlock` variant checked against Google's rules. Their editor blocks come later; until then these types are still written through a post's custom schema field.

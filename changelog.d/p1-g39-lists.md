### Added

- A site can have several newsletters (task 5.7, KH-GRO-2). Each folder in `content/newsletters/`, such as `content/newsletters/weekly/`, is one newsletter beside `main`, and `::newsletter{label="..." button="..." list="weekly"}` signs readers up for it. A form that names a newsletter the site does not have saves nothing and says so. `docs/newsletters.md` explains it.

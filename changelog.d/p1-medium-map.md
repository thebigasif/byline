### Added

- A Medium import maps every old address of each published post, on medium.com, on a publication's own domain and at Medium's short `/p/` address, to its new one, and writes the moves to `import/medium-redirects.csv`. An old path on the new site's own host is answered by Khalid itself through the post's aliases, and each author gets a file with their Medium profile (KH-API-7).

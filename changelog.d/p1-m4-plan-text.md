### Added

- `khalid plan`, the publishing planner: it suggests a day for each draft the linter passes, at the site's own pace over the last twelve weeks or `--per-week <count>`, puts each topic group's pillar before its members, keeps the days already scheduled, lists the drafts that wait and why, and warns when fewer posts went live in the last four weeks than the four before. It changes nothing; `--json` gives the same plan to agents.

### Added

- `site.toml` takes the site's menus as `[[nav.primary]]` and `[[nav.footer]]` links, each with a `label`, an `href` and an optional `language`, and one `[announcement]` line with an optional link. `[reader]` gains `linked_mentions` and `link_previews`, both on by default. A label, line or link a page could not show is refused with its line (G19, first part).

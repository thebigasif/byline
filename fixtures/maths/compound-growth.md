## How traffic compounds

If a post gains $r$ more visits each month, it has $v_n = v_0 (1 + r)^n$ visits after $n$ months.

$$
v_n = v_0 \left( 1 + r \right)^n
$$

Ten posts that each earn $\frac{v}{2}$ beat one post that earns $3v$ when $10 \cdot \frac{v}{2} > 3v$.

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{10} \frac{v}{2} = 5v \geq 3v
$$

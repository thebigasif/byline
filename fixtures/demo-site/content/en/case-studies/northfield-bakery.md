Northfield Bakery is a fictional three-person bakery we use as a worked example. It sold bread online but its blog was a list of unrelated recipes. We planned one [topic cluster](/blog/planning-a-topic-cluster/) on baking sourdough at home: one main post, six short posts, every one linked to the main post.

Four months later, search visits had doubled, and most new visitors arrived on the short posts and went on to the main one.

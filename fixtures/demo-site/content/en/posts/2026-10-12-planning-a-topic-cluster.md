A topic cluster is a group of posts on one subject: one main post that covers the whole subject, and several shorter posts that each answer one question in depth. They all link to each other. Together they show search engines that your site knows the subject, which is what [[topical-authority]] means.

## Plan it on one page

1. **Pick the subject your customers ask about most.** For a bakery that sells online, that might be "sourdough at home".
2. **Write the main question at the top:** "How do I bake sourdough at home?" That becomes the main post.
3. **List the smaller questions underneath:** how to feed a starter, why the dough is sticky, which flour to buy. Each becomes a short post.
4. **Draw the links.** Every short post links to the main post, and the main post links to every short post.

## Write in the right order

Write the short posts first. They are quicker, and they teach you what the main post needs to say. Then write the main post and add the links. [Why internal links matter](/blog/why-internal-links-matter/) has the rules for the anchor text.

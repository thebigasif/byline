The title tag is the line of text a search engine shows as the clickable headline of your result. It is often the first thing a reader sees of your site, so it decides whether they visit at all.

## Five rules

1. **Put the topic first.** "Title tags: how to write one" beats "A complete and friendly guide to title tags".
2. **Keep it under about 60 characters,** so it is not cut off on a phone.
3. **Make it match the page.** If the title promises a checklist, the page needs a checklist. A mismatch sends readers straight back.
4. **Write one per page.** Two pages with the same title compete with each other.
5. **Add your brand at the end, if there is room.** "Title tags: how to write one | Field Notes".

## Title tag or headline?

They can differ. The headline on the page can be longer and warmer. The title tag is for the results page, where space is short and the reader is scanning.

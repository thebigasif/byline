An internal link is a link from one page on your site to another page on the same site. It sounds too simple to matter. It matters a lot.

## Readers use them to keep going

Someone who finishes a post is interested. If the post ends without pointing anywhere, they leave. One link to a related post gives them a next step, and gives you a second page view.

## Search engines use them to find and rank pages

A crawler finds new pages by following links. A post that nothing links to is hard to find and looks unimportant. A post that ten other posts link to looks like something your site cares about. That is how you build [[topical-authority]].

## How to add them well

- Link from new posts to older ones, and go back to add links from older posts to new ones.
- Use anchor text that says what the reader gets: "how to write a title tag", not "click here".
- Use the same anchor text for the same page. If one phrase points to two different pages, search engines cannot tell which one you mean.

Start with one link per post. For example, once your links are in place, read [how to write a title tag](/blog/how-to-write-a-title-tag/) next.

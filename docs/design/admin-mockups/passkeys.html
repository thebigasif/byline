Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your device's PIN, instead of a password. Your phone or laptop keeps it, and it works only on this blog, so nobody can trick you into using it on a fake page.

Add a passkey on this device

Your browser will ask you to unlock this device. After that, you can sign in here with this device alone.

So you can tell your passkeys apart, such as the device it lives on.

Your passkeys

Remove a passkey when you lose or give away the device it lives on. Removing one works at once. Your password and email sign-in links keep working.

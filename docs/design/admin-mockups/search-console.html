Search Console

What Google Search Console, Google's report of how your blog does in search, says about your posts. Khalid asks Google with your own Google account each time you open this page, so it can take a few seconds.

Questions your posts do not answer yet

5 of 12 long searches that showed your posts from 3 October to 30 October 2026 have no heading that answers them.

A long search has 8 words or more. People ask AI assistants in whole questions like these, and each one is a question a reader brings to your post.

To answer one, add a heading to the post that asks the question, with a 40 to 60 word answer under it. Google and AI assistants can then quote that passage.

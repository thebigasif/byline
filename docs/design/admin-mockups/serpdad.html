Connect to SerpDad

SerpDad is the SEO tool that makes this blog. When it is connected, SerpDad sends each finished article here as a draft. Khalid checks the draft on arrival, and it waits for you to publish it. Nothing is sent to SerpDad from here.

What you see first: the connection is off

Turn on drafts from SerpDad

Turning it on makes a key: a long password just for SerpDad. The key lets SerpDad add drafts and nothing else. It cannot publish, delete posts or change settings.

You copy the key into SerpDad once. You can turn the connection off at any time, and SerpDad is refused from then on.

Right after you turn it on: the key, shown once

Copy these two into SerpDad

In SerpDad, open Integrations, choose "Your blog, by SerpDad", and paste them in.

Blog address
https://www.example.com
Key
The key appears here, with a Copy button beside it.

You see this key only once. Khalid keeps a scrambled copy that nobody can turn back into the key. If you lose it, make a new one: the old one stops working.

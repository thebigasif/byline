Emoji, photos and GIFs, and turning or cropping a picture

The last writing aids in the editor. Type a colon to add an emoji by its name. Find a free photo on Unsplash or a GIF on GIPHY without leaving the post. Turn or crop a picture from your media library.

1. An emoji by its name

Type a colon and two letters of a name. Enter adds the emoji itself to the text; Esc leaves what you typed.

2. Photo and GIF in the block menu

They appear only when the site owner has turned them on. Until then nothing in the editor mentions them.

  • GIF A moving picture from GIPHY

3. Finding a photo

The box says where the photo comes from and what goes under it before you pick one.

Photo from Unsplash

Photos stay on Unsplash and load from there. The photographer's name and a link go under the photo, as Unsplash asks.

After a click the post has the photo, with "Photo by Ana Silva on Unsplash" under it. The GIF box works the same way, with "Via GIPHY".

4. Turning or cropping a picture

Open a picture's details, as for any block, and press "Turn or crop". The preview changes as you choose.

Turn or crop the picture

Saving makes a new copy in the media library and puts it in this post. The original stays, so other posts that use it do not change.

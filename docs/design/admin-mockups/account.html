Your account

How you sign in to this blog's admin, and how to lock everyone else out if a laptop goes missing.

Add a passkey

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your device's PIN instead of a password. It cannot be guessed or stolen by a fake sign-in page. Your device asks you to confirm, then keeps the passkey.

So you can tell your passkeys apart, such as Work laptop.

To stop a passkey working, delete it from the device that keeps it.

Change your password

Changing it signs you out on every other device.

At least 12 characters. A short sentence is easy to remember.

Sign out everywhere

For a lost laptop or a phone someone else picked up. Every device signed in as you is signed out, this one too, and you sign in again.
